How this works

A transparent, revenue-multiple approach in the range third-party valuation and financing firms use. The base and each adjustment are set in one config block so the methodology can be tuned to Booth's own analytics.

  • Base multiple of 2.7× gross revenue, the midpoint of published RIA revenue multiples (deals commonly clear 2.5×–3.5× of recurring revenue).
  • Recurring / advisory share is the largest driver. Firms with 90%+ fee-based revenue command a meaningful premium over commission-heavy books.
  • Average client size (AUM ÷ households) rewards larger, stickier, more scalable relationships.
  • Average realized fee (revenue ÷ AUM) rewards a healthy fee band and flags both underpricing and fee-pressure risk.
  • Scale (total AUM) reflects the premium larger firms attract from acquirers and capital partners.
  • Each driver moves smoothly. Adjustments interpolate between the set points, so a firm at 87% recurring lands between the 80% and 90% marks rather than jumping at a threshold.
For estimate only. This tool returns an indicative range based on the figures entered and standard market multiples. An actual valuation depends on growth, client demographics, margin, revenue concentration, and other factors, and may differ materially. It is not an offer, appraisal, or advice.
